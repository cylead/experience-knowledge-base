# Who commissioned a work is key to understanding it

## Claim

A key perspective for understanding differences in architecture—and artworks more generally—is to ask who commissioned the work.

## Why It Matters

> [!note] Agent-organized interpretation
> This approach directs attention beyond visual style toward the person or institution for whom the work came into being.
