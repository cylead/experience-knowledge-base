# The few things we do each day should be challenging and valuable

## Claim

Rather than doing many things each day, we should select a small number that are both challenging and valuable.

## Original Observation

Source capture: [[Four Thousand Weeks#^capture-20260910-four-thousand-weeks-01]]

![[Four Thousand Weeks#^capture-20260910-four-thousand-weeks-01]]

## Why It Matters

> [!note] Agent-organized interpretation
> The reflection evaluates a day by the quality of a few chosen activities rather than by the number completed.

## Evidence / Examples

- The user contrasts doing many things with doing only several things.
- The user says those several things should be challenging and valuable.

## Tensions / Counterexamples

> [!note] Agent-suggested qualification
> Requiring every chosen activity to be challenging and valuable could create pressure that conflicts with preserving time to clear the mind. The reflection leaves “valuable” and the appropriate level of challenge undefined.
