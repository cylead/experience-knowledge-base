# Basic needs become harder to protect as their competitors get stronger

## Claim

Meeting a basic need can become harder even when the need itself has not changed, because newer and more attractive activities increasingly compete for the same time and attention.

## Original Observation

Source capture: [[Stolen Focus#^capture-20260828-stolen-focus-01]]

## Why It Matters

> [!note] Agent-organized interpretation
> This frames difficulty with sleep partly as a change in the strength and availability of competing options, not as evidence that sleep has become less important.

## Evidence / Examples

- The captured example is sleep competing with a growing number of novel and attractive activities.
