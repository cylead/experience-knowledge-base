# Protect sleep from late-night attention capture

> [!warning] Agent suggestion
> This practice was inferred from the reflection. It has not been adopted and remains in `proposed` status until the user chooses to activate it.

## Rationale

Reduce the strength and availability of the options that compete with sleep, rather than relying only on a late-night decision to stop.

## Cue

A personally chosen evening cutoff before the intended sleep period.

## Smallest Viable Behavior

At the cutoff, put the most attention-grabbing device outside the sleeping area and begin one low-stimulation wind-down activity.

## Success Signal

During the weekly review, reflect on whether the boundary was attempted and whether beginning sleep felt easier. No daily completion log is required.
