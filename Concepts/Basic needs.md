# Basic needs

## Definition

> [!note] Agent-drafted definition
> Recurring requirements whose adequate fulfillment supports physical or psychological functioning.

## Boundaries / Non-examples

- Includes requirements such as sleep whose importance persists even when they are easy to postpone.
- Does not automatically include every preference, desire, or attractive option.

## Relationships

- [[Sleep]] is the example used in the first captured insight.
- Basic needs may still lose [[Attention competition]] to more immediately attractive activities.
