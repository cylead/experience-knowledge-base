# Commissioner

## Definition

> [!note] Agent-drafted definition
> The person or institution that requests or authorizes the creation of a work.

## Boundaries / Non-examples

- The commissioner is not necessarily the architect, artist, or other person who creates the work.
- A later owner or user is not necessarily the commissioner.

## Relationships

- Identifying the commissioner can provide a perspective for interpreting architecture and artwork.
