# Attention competition

## Definition

> [!note] Agent-drafted definition
> The contest among activities and stimuli for a person's limited conscious attention and available time.

## Boundaries / Non-examples

- Describes competing options, cues, and activities rather than attention as an isolated personal trait.
- Does not imply that every attractive activity is harmful; the issue is what it displaces.

## Relationships

- Can make it harder to protect [[Basic needs]].
- In the seed insight, late-day alternatives compete with [[Sleep]].
