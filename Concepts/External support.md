# External support

## Definition

> [!note] Agent-drafted definition
> Material, financial, military, intelligence, diplomatic, or political assistance provided to a participant in a conflict by another actor.

## Boundaries / Non-examples

- External support does not by itself mean that the supporter controls the participant receiving assistance.
- The concept concerns backing for a conflict participant, not every relationship that participant has with other actors.

## Relationships

- Examining external support can reveal actors and relationships beyond the countries visibly fighting on the front.
