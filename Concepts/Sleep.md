# Sleep

## Definition

> [!note] Agent-drafted definition
> A recurring biological state of rest required for recovery, functioning, and well-being.

## Boundaries / Non-examples

- Includes the need for sufficient and regular sleep, not merely time spent in bed.
- Does not mean every form of passive entertainment or inactivity.

## Relationships

- A concrete example of [[Basic needs]].
- Can be displaced when other activities win [[Attention competition]] late in the day.
