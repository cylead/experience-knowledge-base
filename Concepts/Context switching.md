# Context switching

## Definition

> [!note] Agent-drafted definition
> Moving attention between different tasks, topics, or information contexts, requiring repeated reorientation.

## Boundaries / Non-examples

> [!note] Agent-organized boundaries
> - A change of context is not necessarily harmful; its effects depend on factors such as frequency, purpose, and the effort required to reorient.
> - Linear progression within one text is not context switching merely because the subject develops or changes.

## Relationships

> [!note] Agent-suggested relationship
> Context switching may interact with [[Attention competition]] when competing links, topics, or stimuli pull a reader toward another context.
