# Spare time

## Definition

> [!note] Agent-drafted definition
> Time not committed to an immediate obligation, including brief intervals between planned activities.

## Boundaries / Non-examples

- Spare time does not have to be productive to be used well; intentional rest can be a meaningful use of it.
- The concept describes available time, not a requirement to fill every moment with an activity.

## Relationships

- When its use is not chosen deliberately, [[Spare time]] can become a site of [[Attention competition]].
